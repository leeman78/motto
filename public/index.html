Our story

We are Motto.

Ten years of putting tech accessories on American counters, from a warehouse in the Dallas metro.

Motto was founded in 2016 on a simple commitment: top-quality tech accessories that make charging feel better than it has any right to.

Premium leather charging cables have been at the core of that from the beginning. Every accessory rack in the country carries the same black nylon braid at the same price, so we built something a customer notices from three feet away and still pays under fifteen dollars for. It is still our best-turning line, and it is the reason most partners call us in the first place.

We grew where most accessory brands will not go: independent gas stations and convenience stores, one door at a time. That is a demanding place to sell. Shelf space is measured in inches, the buyer is usually the owner, and a product that does not turn is gone in a month. It taught us to build for the peg rather than the product page.

Today you will find Motto at gas stations across Texas, Louisiana, Oklahoma and Arkansas, and the assortment has grown to cover cables, wall and car chargers, wired and true wireless audio, and adapters. We keep innovating on the line because the counter does not stand still.
