Cookie Settings

Last updated August 14, 2026

This site does not run advertising trackers, analytics pixels or third-party marketing scripts. There is nothing here to opt out of, so we have not built a consent banner with toggles that would do nothing. What we do use is browser storage, and this page lists every item of it.

Our retail store is different. mottousa.com runs on a different platform with analytics and advertising cookies, and it has its own consent banner with Essential, Analytics and Personalization options. Choices you make there do not carry over here, and nothing here is shared with it.

Clear what is stored

This removes the preference and cart entries above from this browser. It does not delete your account or your order history, and it will sign you out if you are signed in.

If this changes

If we ever add analytics or advertising to this site, we will add a consent banner before switching it on, not after, and this page will list what changed. Until then a banner here would be theatre.

Your browser controls

You can block or clear site storage from your browser settings at any time. If you do, the cart and your view preference will not persist and you will need to sign in again.
